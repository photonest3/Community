Powerful features for your photo gallery

Upload your photos

You can add a folder. If you run it again, only new photos will be uploaded.

Batch Manager

Filter photos from your collection, make a selection and apply actions in batch: change the author, add some tags, associate to a new album...

Organize with Album

The album tree has unlimited depth. Each photo can belong to several albums.

Browse by Dates

Digital cameras store the date in the photo and uses this date to display your photo collection in a calendar and let you browse from day to day.

Privacy on your Photos

Make your photos private and encrypted storage.

Browse by GPS

It can read GPS latitude and longitude from embedded metadata. Then, display your photo collection.
